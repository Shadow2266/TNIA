Our Association

Bringing Nursing & Informatics Together

In the social, physiological, and economic health of healthcare systems, nurses serve as pivotal coordinators and drivers of change. TNIA integrates nursing science with information technology to achieve holistic, patient-centered care.

Mission Statement
To advance nursing practice through information technology, fostering compassionate care, operational efficiency, and continuous innovation.
Aims & Objectives

Advancing Healthcare Through Informatics

To advance the nursing informatics profession, promote the development and application of information technology in healthcare, and foster international academic exchanges.

TNIA acts as an open systems organization connecting clinical care, administrative management, education, quality control, and expert decision-support systems.

Vision

To lead the future of intelligent nursing by integrating emerging technologies with compassionate, person-centered care.

Historical Journey (2006–2026)

20 Years of Groundbreaking Milestones

From establishing Taiwan’s first nursing informatics society to national policy execution and global leadership.

18 June 2006

TNIA Foundation & Inaugural Assembly

Following calls by Prof. Polun Chang in 2005, TNIA was formally inaugurated at Taipei Veterans General Hospital Zhide Building. Adopted the official charter, elected the 1st Board of Directors led by President Hsiu-Chih Chen, and unveiled the IT emblem.

Founding Milestone · 300+ Inaugural Members
June 2010

Admission to Alliance of Nursing Informatics (ANI) — First in Asia

During the International Nursing Informatics Workshop in Taipei, Prof. Connie Delaney formally presented TNIA with certification of admission into ANI (representing over 5,000 informatics nurses worldwide). Taiwan became the first Asian member country in ANI.

International Expansion · Taipei Roundtable
2010

Initiation of the Taiwan TIGER Initiative

Invited Dr. Patricia H. Walker to Taiwan to deliver a keynote on the “Technology Informatics Guiding Educational Reform (TIGER)” initiative at Wan Fang Hospital, formulating guidelines across 7 dimensions (Leadership, Education, Communication, Design, IT, Policy, Culture).

Educational Reform · Competency Guidelines
2013–2015

Entrusted with the National EMR Project Office (衛生署電子病歷專案辦公室)

TNIA was commissioned by the Department of Health to oversee the National Electronic Medical Record Project Office and facilitate nationwide EMR interoperability initiatives. Established cross-hospital EMR exchange standards, verified 208 hospitals on-site, and enabled 150 institutions for cross-hospital EMR data exchange.

National Health Policy Execution · Standard Infrastructure
2014

Host Nation for NI 2014 Conference & Gold Award

Hosted the International Conference on Nursing Informatics (NI 2014) at Taipei TICC with the theme “eSMART+”. Over 84 research papers published, attracting world-class experts. Honored with the National International Conference Gold Quality Award (經建會金質獎).

International Academic Achievement · Gold Quality Award
2015

Inauguration of the National Informatics Nurse Certification System

Following research on the role and feasibility of informatics nurses in Taiwan, TNIA established a formal dual-track credentialing system (Credit Evaluation & Examination) across Foundational and Advanced tiers. Through 2025, certified 514 Foundational and 39 Advanced Informatics Nurses.

Professional Credentialing · Bi-annual Examinations
2017–2023

Biennial Smart Nursing Innovation Awards (智慧護理競賽)

Launched biennial national smart nursing competitions to stimulate creative clinical IT integration. Expanded in 2021 to include healthcare IT industry vendors. Past Grand Champions include Kaohsiung VGH (Controlled drugs “e-route”), En Chu Kong Hospital (Chemotherapy re-engineering), and Taipei Tzu Chi Hospital (Cloud smart room platform).

Innovation Achievement · Industry & Hospital Partnership
9 July 2024

Cross-Strait Healthcare Standards MOU

Signed an academic and standards collaboration MOU with Xiamen Cardiovascular Hospital to co-develop technical guidelines: NI Core Competencies Framework, Talent Training Specifications, Hospital Nursing Informatization Architecture, and Attendant-free Care Models.

Standardization Exchange · Technical Specifications
August 2025

MEDINFO 2025 World Congress in Taipei

Co-organized the 20th World Congress on Medical and Health Informatics (MEDINFO 2025) in Taipei. TNIA leadership served on the Local Organizing Committee (LOC) and hosted specialized nursing informatics tracks showcasing cutting-edge AI and clinical workflows.

International Recognition · World Health Informatics Congress
2026

20th Anniversary & Launch of the PHOENIX 2036 AI Decade

Celebrating two decades of excellence with the 20th Anniversary Commemorative Special Issue and officially launching the PHOENIX (2026–2036) Plan to empower nurses through Artificial Intelligence and next-generation care networks.

20th Milestone · PHOENIX 2026–2036 Plan
Professional Credentialing

Informatics Nurse Certification & Training

Building the standard of excellence for clinical nursing informatics in Taiwan through standardized credentialing, TIGER competencies, and hands-on workshops.

514 Foundational Informatics Nurses Certified
39 Advanced Informatics Nurses Certified

Dual-Track Assessment

TNIA conducts certification twice annually via two established routes:

  • Examination Track: Standardized written examination evaluating health data management, informatics standards, and clinical workflows.
  • Credit Evaluation Track: Verification of accredited university/continuing education coursework and verified hospital informatics project leadership.

Two-Tier Hierarchy

Structured competency progression from unit champion to hospital enterprise leadership:

  • Foundational Level: Mastering clinical documentation systems, data quality, electronic chart security, and unit-level workflow optimization.
  • Advanced Level: Leading interdisciplinary AI deployments, system design evaluation, hospital-wide EHR integration, and big-data analytics.
Curriculum Foundation

The Taiwan TIGER Educational Framework

TNIA training courses are modeled after the international TIGER (Technology Informatics Guiding Educational Reform) 7 core pillars:

1. Leadership

Transformational leadership driving digital health transitions.

2. Education

Learning communities disseminating rapid best practices.

3. Collaboration

Standardized data fostering team coordination and continuity.

4. Informatics Design

Evidence-based, interactive smart systems for patient safety.

5. Information Tech

Human-centered, interoperable, and affordable technologies.

6. Policy & Culture

Incentive-based policies supporting open safety cultures.

7. AI & Innovation

Next-gen smart hospital systems and AI clinical decision aids.

🔥 Next Decade Strategic Vision (2026–2036)

The PHOENIX Plan (Under the Leadership of Professor Polun Chang)

PHOENIX is a conceptual acronym representing the strategic transformation of nursing informatics through artificial intelligence and digital innovation. After 20 years of foundational refinement, TNIA stands mature and ready to embrace the AI-empowered era. Proposed in 2026, the PHOENIX Initiative outlines our strategic blueprint for the next decade (2026–2036).

1. Ensuring People's Health with AI-empowered Nurses

Leveraging Artificial Intelligence, automated ambient voice charting, and smart decision-support systems to alleviate clinical workload, reduce cognitive burden, and empower nurses to refocus on compassionate, high-touch patient care.

2. People's Health Outreach agEnt for Nursing Informatics neXt

Creating next-generation intelligent nursing informatics platforms, extending smart clinical decision agents from tertiary medical centers into regional clinics, long-term care facilities, and community health networks.

Core Focus: User-centric Systems · EMR Interoperability · Mobile & Ambient AI Care
